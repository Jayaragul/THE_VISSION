Nvidia is reportedly nearing a deal to buy Hugging Face, but nothing is signed

The Information reported Nvidia "agreed to buy" the open-model hub for roughly $13 billion, while Business Insider says talks could still collapse; neither company has commented.

THE VISSION Desk
Friday, 28 August 2026 2 min read 2 sources Credible reporting, no primary

Original cover art, generated for this story. THE VISSION does not republish third-party press imagery.

The short version
  • The Information reported Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for approximately $12.9–13 billion.
  • Business Insider, reporting separately, said talks "had not yet produced a signed agreement and could still fall apart."
  • Neither Nvidia nor Hugging Face has issued a public statement confirming or denying a deal.
  • Hugging Face hosts the largest public repository of open-weight AI models and datasets; an acquisition by Nvidia would hand a chipmaker unusual influence over the software layer much of the open-source AI ecosystem depends on.
Corrected after publication

Friday, 28 August 2026 — Business Insider was quoted as saying the talks could still "atomize". The reported wording is "fall apart"; the quotation has been corrected. The desk's error in drafting — a word that was never said should not have appeared inside quotation marks.

Friday, 28 August 2026 — The TechCrunch citation pointed at a URL that returns 404. The article is "Nvidia closes in on Hugging Face acquisition"; the link and the cited headline have been corrected to match it. Caught by the link check, not by review.

Nvidia is reportedly close to acquiring Hugging Face, the platform that hosts the largest public library of open-weight AI models, according to The Information, which put the price at roughly $12.9 to $13 billion and said the two companies had already agreed to the deal. Business Insider, reporting separately, offered a more cautious account: talks were advanced but had "not yet produced a signed agreement and could still fall apart."

Neither company has confirmed the talks publicly. TechCrunch, which reviewed both reports, noted the discrepancy directly rather than resolving it, and no signed agreement, deal terms, or closing timeline have been independently verified as of publication.

If it happens, the acquisition would be notable less for the price than for what Hugging Face represents: the default distribution point for open-weight models from Meta, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen and dozens of smaller labs, plus the datasets and tooling much of the research community builds on. Nvidia already supplies the hardware most of that ecosystem trains and runs on; owning the hub it's distributed through would be a different kind of leverage.

Why it matters

Readers should treat this as an unconfirmed report, not a completed acquisition — two outlets differ on whether an agreement actually exists, and neither company will say. But even as a rumor, the number is a useful marker of what infrastructure players think open-model distribution is now worth, and the strategic logic — a chipmaker controlling the platform independent labs rely on to reach users — would matter regardless of who ends up owning it.
