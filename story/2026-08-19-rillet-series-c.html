AI accounting startup Rillet raises $100 million, reaching a $1 billion valuation

The short version
  • Rillet, a two-year-old AI-native accounting platform, raised a $100 million Series C at a $1 billion valuation, Fortune reported August 18.
  • The round was led by ICONIQ, with returning investors Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz and Oak HC/FT joined by new backers including Bain Capital Ventures, Battery Ventures and FirstMark.
