Deep Cogito raises $43 million to keep pushing self-improving open models

The Series A, led by TQ Ventures with Zscaler joining as both investor and customer, brings the post-training startup's total funding past $56 million.

THE VISSION Desk
Friday, 28 August 2026 1 min read 1 source Credible reporting, no primary

Original cover art, generated for this story. THE VISSION does not republish third-party press imagery.

The short version
  • Deep Cogito raised a $43 million Series A led by TQ Ventures, with Benchmark, Nexus Venture Partners, Atreides Management, South Park Commons and Zscaler participating.
  • Total funding to date exceeds $56 million; the company was founded in 2024 by former Google AI Search engineers Drishan Arora and Dhruv Malrana.
  • The startup's post-training engine underlies its open-weight Cogito model family, ranging from 3 billion to more than 600 billion parameters.
  • Zscaler, one of the round's investors, is also a customer using the platform to build custom models on its own proprietary data.
