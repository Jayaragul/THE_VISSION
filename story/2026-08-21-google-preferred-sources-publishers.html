Google gives publishers a button that lets readers pin them in Search and Discover

More than 345,000 sources have been selected since May, and Google says readers click through to a preferred source about twice as often.

THE VISSION Desk

The short version
  • Google released an embeddable "Preferred Sources" button that publishers can place on their own sites, letting a reader mark them as a favourite across Search, Discover and Google News.
  • Google says readers are about twice as likely to click through to a source they have marked as preferred.
  • More than 345,000 unique sources have been selected since the feature launched in May, previously reachable only through Google's own preferences page.
  • The company positions it as a response to publishers losing traffic as AI-generated answers resolve queries without a click.
