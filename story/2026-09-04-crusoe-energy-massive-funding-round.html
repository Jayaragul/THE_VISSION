Crusoe reportedly raises $3 billion, valuing cloud startup at $30 billion

The massive funding round reflects the intense demand for GPU cloud infrastructure powered by stranded and renewable energy sources.

Friday, 4 September 2026 1 min read 1 source Credible reporting, no primary

The short version
  • AI cloud infrastructure provider Crusoe has reportedly raised a massive $3 billion funding round on September 3, 2026.
  • The transaction values the company at $30 billion, representing a significant jump from its previous capital valuations.
  • Crusoe operates data centers powered by stranded natural gas flaring and renewable energy to lower AI carbon footprints.

AI cloud infrastructure provider Crusoe has reportedly raised a massive new funding round of $3 billion, valuing the company at $30 billion, according to reports from TechCrunch on September 3, 2026. The oversubscribed capital round highlights the aggressive investor appetite for specialized GPU computing clusters as frontier AI model training scales up.

Crusoe originally built its business by deploying modular data centers directly to oil fields to harvest stranded natural gas flaring, converting waste energy into low-cost electricity for bitcoin mining and AI computing. More recently, the company has pivoted heavily into GPU cloud hosting, partnering with developers to build massive data center campuses powered by renewable wind, solar, and geothermal energy to support high-performance computing.

The $3 billion raise will be used to purchase tens of thousands of next-generation NVIDIA GPUs and build out new data center capacity. Neither Crusoe nor its primary investors have officially disclosed the full syndicate of the round. However, the reported $30 billion valuation marks Crusoe as one of the most valuable privately-held infrastructure providers in the global technology ecosystem.

Why it matters

Crusoe's massive $30 billion valuation reflects a broader shift where the physical ownership of energy and compute infrastructure is becoming just as valuable as model development. As the AI sector faces mounting scrutiny over its immense carbon footprint and grid capacity demands, Crusoe's green-energy, waste-gas harvesting model provides a crucial release valve for developers seeking large-scale compute without the associated political and environmental backlash.
