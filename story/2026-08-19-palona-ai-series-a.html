Palona AI raises $20 million to bring AI automation to restaurants and retail

The Series A, led by Ardenwood Ventures, funds a vision-AI platform already deployed at chains including Cali BBQ to monitor operations and coordinate frontline teams.

The short version
  • Palona AI raised a $20 million Series A led by Ardenwood Ventures, with participation from Fusion Fund, Neo and five other investors, SiliconANGLE reported August 17.
  • The platform uses vision AI and object detection to monitor restaurant and retail operations in real time and coordinate actions across staff, software and AI agents.
  • Palona, which emerged from stealth in January 2025 after a $10 million seed round, counts the Cali BBQ restaurant chain among its deployments.
