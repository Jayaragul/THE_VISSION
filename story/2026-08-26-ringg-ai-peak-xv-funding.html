Bengaluru's Ringg AI raises $10 million as it pushes voice agents past the phone call

The Series A extension, led by Peak XV, brings the company's total funding to $15.5 million; it already handles 20 million call attempts a month for Cred, Flipkart and Practo.

The short version
  • Ringg AI, a Bengaluru voice-AI startup, raised a $10 million Series A extension led by Peak XV Partners, bringing its total Series A to $15.5 million.
  • The company processes 20 million call attempts monthly, automating customer support, appointment booking, abandoned-cart recovery and loan collection for clients including Cred, Flipkart, Practo, Groww, Policybazaar and Shell.

  • The company has grown to 40 employees, with more than 15 hired in the past three months, and is expanding from voice into chat, WhatsApp and browser-based support.

Ringg AI, a Bengaluru voice-AI startup, has raised a $10 million Series A extension led by Peak XV Partners, bringing its total Series A funding to $15.5 million. The company processes 20 million call attempts a month, automating tasks like appointment booking for healthcare providers, abandoned-cart recovery for e-commerce, KYC checks for fintech companies, and loan collection — work drawing on named clients including Cred, Flipkart, Practo, Groww, Policybazaar and Shell.

The company did not start as a voice-agent business. It launched as DesiVocal, a text-to-speech platform, before pivoting toward enterprise voice AI agents that complete tasks rather than simply converse. Co-founder Siddharth Tripathi described the shift in framing directly: "We are trying to position ourselves as a platform for agents that bring outcomes or get things done rather than voice agents."

Ringg has grown to 40 employees, more than 15 of them hired in the past three months, and is extending beyond phone calls into chat, WhatsApp and browser-based support — the "past the phone call" expansion the round is meant to fund. Founders Siddharth Tripathi, Kali CV and Utkarsh Shukla lead the company.

Why it matters

Ringg's named client list — Cred, Flipkart, Practo, Groww, Policybazaar — is a cross-section of India's largest consumer platforms already routing real customer interactions through a voice agent, which is a more concrete adoption signal than most enterprise-AI funding announcements carry. For builders watching the Indian market specifically, a company originating in text-to-speech and pivoting to outcome-based voice agents is a template other regional AI startups are likely to follow rather than a one-off.
