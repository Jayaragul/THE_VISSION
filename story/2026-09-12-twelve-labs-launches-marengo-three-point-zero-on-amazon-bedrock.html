Twelve Labs launches Marengo Embed 3.0 video model on Amazon Bedrock

Marengo becomes the first video-specific embedding model integrated into Bedrock's fully managed knowledge base service, enabling natural language visual search.

THE VISSION Desk
Saturday, 12 September 2026 1 min read 2 sources Credible reporting, no primary

The short version
  • Twelve Labs has announced the general availability of its Marengo Embed 3.0 model as a managed service within Amazon Bedrock.
  • The model is the first video-specific embedding tool on Bedrock, encoding visual scenes, speech, and audio into unified 512-dimensional vectors.
  • The integration allows enterprise AWS customers to build precise semantic search across video libraries using natural language queries.
